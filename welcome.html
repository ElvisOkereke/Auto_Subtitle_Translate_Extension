Let's get this computer ready

A few minutes now, so captions start the moment you want them. Speech recognition and subtitle reading run on this computer.

  1. What do you watch?

  2. Download and prepare

    Checking your graphics card…

    Turn on hardware acceleration for faster, more accurate captions.

    1. Open the browser's settings, then System.
    2. Turn on Use graphics acceleration when available.
    3. Relaunch the browser and open this page again from the extension's Settings.
    Speech model for Audio mode
    • Speech recognition for Audio mode Not prepared

    • Subtitle reader for Subtitles mode Not prepared

    Keep this tab open until both are ready. You can keep browsing in other tabs.

    All set. Captions will start in seconds.

  3. Use it on any video

    Subtitles in the video

    1. Open a video and click the extension's icon. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu to keep it handy.
    2. Choose Subtitles, then Draw subtitle box.
    3. Drag a box over where the subtitles appear. A rough box is fine.

    Speech in the video

    1. Open a video and click the extension's icon.
    2. Choose Audio, then Start live captions.
    3. Captions appear on the video as people speak.

    How translations are made, and with which service, is in the extension's Settings.
